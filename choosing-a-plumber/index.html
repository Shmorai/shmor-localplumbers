Questions to ask

  • Have you done this type of job before? Can I see examples or references?
  • Is your quote fixed, or an estimate? What could change it?
  • Who will do the work: you, an employee or a subcontractor?
  • What guarantee or warranty comes with the work and the parts?
  • Will you handle any certification, notification or permits?

Red flags

  • Cold calls or door-knocking offering urgent work.
  • Pressure to decide on the spot, or "today only" prices.
  • Cash-only with no receipt, or a large payment before work starts.
  • No landline, address or business details.
  • Unwilling to show a registration card or licence number.

Frequently asked questions

Are online reviews reliable?
They help, but look for a pattern across several sites, read recent reviews and check how the site verifies them. Combine reviews with registration and licence checks.
Do plumbers need a licence in the UK?
There's no general licence for plumbing in the UK, but gas work must be done by a Gas Safe registered engineer, and some water and building work must meet regulations and be certified.
