About LocalPlumbers

How LocalPlumbers works

LocalPlumbers helps you compare your options and get introduced to plumbers in your area. Here's exactly what we do, what we don't, and how we make money.

Find a plumber in your area →

Photo: Kelsey Todd on Unsplash

Four steps

  1. Choose your area

    UK or USA first, then your region, nation or state.

  2. Describe the job

    Pick the job type and how soon you need help.

  3. Compare routes

    See quote partners for your area, registers and guides.

  4. Check and book

    Check credentials, compare written quotes, then book.

What we do

  • Organise plumbing advice by country and region so it applies where you live.
  • Compare the main ways to find a plumber: quote marketplaces, review directories, official registers and local firms.
  • Introduce you to quote partners that cover your area, when we have them.

What we don't do

  • We don't employ, train, vet or guarantee plumbers.
  • We don't publish made-up prices, ratings or reviews.
  • We don't collect your address. The optional town or postcode in the finder stays in your browser.

How we make money

Some links go to partners who may pay us a referral fee if you use their service. That never changes what you pay. Partner links are marked and use our Shmor.ai affiliate disclosure. LocalPlumbers is part of the Shmor.ai network.
