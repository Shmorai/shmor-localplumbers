How the finder works

The finder asks four things: your country, your region, nation or state, the job and how soon you need help. Your town or postcode is optional and stays in your browser.

You then get a plan with three parts: how to get introduced to plumbers in your area, the checks to make before you book, and the guides for your job.

Regions we cover

UK: London, South East, South West, East of England, East Midlands, West Midlands, Yorkshire and the Humber, North West, North East, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland.

USA: all 50 states and the District of Columbia, grouped by Northeast, Midwest, South and West.

Frequently asked questions

Will plumbers contact me straight away?
Not from the finder itself. It shows you your options. You only share details with a plumber or quote service if you choose to contact them.
Can I search for an emergency plumber?
Yes. Choose "Emergency: today" and your plan starts with the safety steps to take first, including gas emergency numbers for your area.
