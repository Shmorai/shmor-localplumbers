Prices and quotes

What affects the cost of a plumber?

Plumbing prices vary widely by area, firm and job. Rather than guess at figures, here's what drives the price and how to make sure quotes are comparable.

The main things that affect price

FactorWhy it matters
Call-out or trip chargeSome plumbers charge to attend, sometimes deducted from the job.
Hourly vs fixed priceHourly suits diagnosis and small jobs; fixed prices suit defined work like installs.
Time of dayEvenings, weekends and holidays are often charged at a higher rate.
Parts and materialsFittings, valves, boilers and water heaters vary a lot in price and quality.
AccessPipes under floors, behind tiles or in tight spaces take longer.
LocationLabour rates differ between cities and rural areas, and between regions and states.
Permits and certificationUS permits and UK certification or notification can add to the cost.
TaxUK quotes may or may not include VAT; in the USA sales tax on parts varies by state.

Comparing quotes fairly

  • Get at least three written quotes for planned work.
  • Make sure each quote covers the same scope, parts and finish.
  • Ask what happens if they find extra problems once work starts.
  • Check guarantees on labour and warranties on parts.
  • The cheapest quote isn't always the best value. Check registration, licence and insurance too.

Your rights

UK: under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, a service must be carried out with reasonable care and skill, and for a reasonable price if none was agreed. If you agree a contract in your home you usually have a 14-day cancellation period, though urgent repairs you asked for can be an exception.

USA: consumer protection rules vary by state. Your state attorney general's office publishes guidance on hiring contractors.

Frequently asked questions

Why don't you list prices?
Prices change and vary a lot by area and job, and we don't want to show figures that could be out of date or wrong for you. Written quotes from local plumbers are the only reliable guide.
Should I pay a deposit?
For bigger jobs a deposit for materials is common. Avoid paying the full amount up front and get the payment terms in writing.
