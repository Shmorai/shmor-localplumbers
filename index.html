UK or USA: what changes

In the UK, anyone working on gas appliances such as boilers must be on the Gas Safe Register. WaterSafe lists plumbers approved to work on water fittings. See plumbers in the UK.

In the USA, plumbers are usually licensed, mostly by the state and in some places by the city or county. Bigger jobs often need a permit and inspection. See plumbers in the USA.

Frequently asked questions

Is LocalPlumbers free to use?
Yes. The finder and guides are free and you don't need an account. If you use a partner link we may earn a referral fee from that partner, at no extra cost to you.
Are you a plumbing company?
No. LocalPlumbers is a comparison and introduction service. We don't employ, train or vet plumbers, so always check registrations, licences and insurance before work starts.
Why do I have to choose a country and region first?
The rules, registers and quote services are different in the UK and the USA, and they vary by nation and state. Choosing your area first means the advice and introductions you see apply where you live.
Do you store my postcode or ZIP code?
No. The optional town or postcode field stays in your browser for this visit only and isn't sent to us.
What should I do in a plumbing emergency?
Turn off your water at the stopcock (UK) or main shutoff valve (USA). If you smell gas, leave and call the gas emergency number: 0800 111 999 in Great Britain, 0800 002 001 in Northern Ireland, or 911 or your gas utility in the USA. Then see our emergency plumber guide.
